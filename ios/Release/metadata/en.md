**Description:**
Gas & CO Monitor shows readings from your Bluetooth carbon monoxide and gas sensor on iPhone and Apple Watch.

• Two clear gauges – CO and gas – colored by level: safe, elevated, dangerous.
• Alarm: red screen, haptics on the watch and a notification on iPhone.
• Standalone Apple Watch app – connects to the sensor without your iPhone.
• Watch face complications: circular, corner, rectangular and inline. Show CO, gas or both.
• Reading history for the last hour, 6 and 24 hours.
• Automatic connection to your remembered sensor and reconnection when back in range.
• Always On, VoiceOver and larger text support.

Your data stays on your device – the app sends nothing.

IMPORTANT: Requires a compatible Bluetooth sensor (UART, manufacturer ID 0x6969). This app is not a certified safety device and does not replace a standards-compliant carbon monoxide or gas alarm (EN 50291 / EN 50194). Watch complications may show delayed data – always check the time of the last reading.
